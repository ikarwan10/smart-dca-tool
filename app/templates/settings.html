📈 About Tom Lee's DCA Strategy

This strategy increases your investment amount when the market drops below its all-time high. The logic is simple: buy more when prices are lower (on sale). During normal times, invest your base amount. During corrections and crashes, invest 2-3x more to take advantage of lower prices.
